# Pact Win Detection v0.1.0

## What this produces

Client wins found in the board's own record, each **documented where it belongs** — a `WIN:`
note on the most relevant existing bead plus a `win` tag — and a short per-client summary in
chat. Wins are never a new object: no pact is created, closed, or restructured by this skill.

## The bar — what counts as a win

A win is a **client-side outcome, evidenced in the record, that moved the client's real-world
goal forward.** Qualifying shapes:

- A lead, meeting, or deal generated by delivered work ("booked a meeting with a lead from the
  framework").
- Commercial motion by the client: a renewal, an expansion, a referral or intro they made.
- The client using a deliverable **recurrently or unaided** (not the assisted first run).
- The client naming value in their own words — substantive praise, a testimonial, "this saved
  us X".

**Never a win on its own:** our task closed, a deploy shipped, a milestone marked done, a report
sent, a demo given. Delivered ≠ converted — internal completion never qualifies without a
client-side consequence in the record.

When a candidate is genuinely uncertain, report it in chat as a *candidate* and stop there.
Never document an uncertain win: a false `WIN:` note travels into client-facing reports; a
missed one just waits for the next sweep.

## Step 1 — Scope the sweep

Confirm which project board to sweep (the session's project by default; cross-project boards
via `list_beads` with `project:`). Window: since the most recent `WIN:` note on the board, or
the last 14 days if there is none. The user saying "this sprint" or "since the demo" overrides
the window.

## Step 2 — Sweep the record

Collect the beads whose record could hold a win, then read their notes:

1. `list_beads` for the window's activity: recently `done` pacts, in-progress work, and the
   project's goals/milestones (`bead_type: goal,milestone` — client outcomes are usually noted
   at goal level).
2. `get_bead` with `full_notes: true` on each candidate. The win is almost always in a **note**
   — ingestion notes quoting Slack/Teams, sprint-report provenance, operator 1:1 notes — not in
   the bead's status.
3. Read for client-outcome language: booked, scheduled, closed, renewed, referred, signed,
   "client said", a client name attached to a result. Ignore our own delivery verbs (shipped,
   deployed, merged, fixed).

## Step 3 — Classify against the bar

For each candidate, write down before judging: the **evidence** (the note's own words), the
**date**, the **people** (which client, who acted), and the **bead** where it lives. Then apply
the bar above. Three outcomes: win (document it), candidate (chat only, say what's missing),
not a win (drop silently).

## Step 4 — Document, idempotently

For each confirmed win:

1. **Dedupe first.** If any existing `WIN:` note on that bead (or an obviously related bead)
   already covers the same event, skip it — one event, one note, ever. Re-runs must be no-ops.
2. `add_note` on the most relevant bead, in this exact shape:

   ```
   WIN: [YYYY-MM-DD] · [client] — [one-sentence outcome in plain words].
   Evidence: "[shortest quote that proves it]" ([source note date / link if present]).
   Detected by pact-win-detection.
   ```

   The most relevant bead is the one whose work produced the outcome; if none fits, the goal
   the outcome serves. Date is the event's date, not today's.
3. Add the `win` tag to that bead (`update_bead` with tags), keeping its existing tags. This
   makes every win findable in one `list_beads tag=win` call.

## Step 5 — Surface

In chat, one line per win, grouped by client: `[client] — [outcome] ([bead handle], [date])`.
Then candidates, clearly labeled as not documented, with what evidence would confirm each.
If nothing qualified, say so plainly — never pad with near-wins promoted to wins.

Wins feed the stakeholder report: `pact-stakeholder-report` credits `win`-tagged beads and
`WIN:` notes as delivered evidence. When a report for this project is being prepared in the
same session, run this sweep first.

## Cadence

Run on demand, before any stakeholder or sprint report, or as a recurring routine — a weekly
sweep per active project via `pact-loops` is the standard shape. The dedupe rule in Step 4 is
what makes any cadence safe.
